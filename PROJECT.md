# HASH.DEVS — PROJECT BRIEF & CHARTER

## 1. Executive Summary

**HASH.DEVS** is an elite creative technology and digital development studio. The studio builds award-winning web applications, interactive 3D ecosystems, and brand flagships for forward-thinking technology companies, gaming ecosystems, and luxury modern brands.

The studio flagship website represents our technical showcase: a statement of high-velocity engineering, cinematic motion, editorial typography, and digital craftsmanship.

---

## 2. Creative Reference: Trionn Studio

Our primary creative benchmark is **[Trionn](https://trionn.com/)**.

### Benchmark Attributes to Match and Surpass:
1. **Kinetic Fluency:** Smooth, inertia-calibrated scrolling that feels physical and weighted, never sticky or jittery.
2. **Editorial Brutalism meets High Tech:** Bold scale contrast between giant architectural typography and microscopic mono technical readouts.
3. **Intentional Transitions:** Seamless page transitions, skew reveals, text splits, and depth-based parallax.
4. **Performance Rigor:** High frame-rate execution (consistent 60/120fps) with minimal layout thrashing and efficient memory lifecycle.

### Explicit Anti-Patterns (What We Avoid):
- **No generic AI aesthetics:** No purple-to-cyan gradient blobs, neon fog, or generic glassmorphic card overlays.
- **No cookie-cutter templates:** No generic SaaS dashboards or standard hero-section layouts.
- **No arbitrary decorative noise:** Every animation, interaction, and hover state must reinforce hierarchy and communicate state.
- **No unconstrained rounded corners:** Architectural, sharp geometric rhythm with calculated, intentional radii.

---

## 3. Brand Identity Tokens

- **Obsidian Black (`#050505`):** The foundational substrate. Infinite depth, high contrast.
- **Hash Neon Green (`#35E85B`):** Pure electric energy, code prompts, interactive accents, and precision indicators.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-readability headlines, structural rules, and primary content.
- **Graphite (`#171A18`):** Architectural boundary definitions, subtle card borders, and secondary surfaces.
